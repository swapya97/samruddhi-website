Module 4 – Finished Goods Stock
Stock calculation:
\[
 Closing\ Stock = Opening\ Stock + Production - Sales - Rejections \]
Sales and dispatch must be managed separately. Stock should decrease only when goods are actually dispatched, not merely when a customer places an order.
